Revenue

Financial Health in Real Time

Track every dollar flowing through your agency. The executive dashboard surfaces the KPIs that matter - revenue, recurring income, and outstanding payments - with period comparison built in.

  • Total revenue with period-over-period comparison
  • Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) and ARR
  • Outstanding and overdue invoice totals
  • 6-month revenue trend chart
  • Filter by: This Month, Last Month, Quarter, Year

02
Project Health

Spot At-Risk Projects Before They Derail

Every active project is automatically scored based on task completion and deadline proximity. Green means on track. Yellow means at risk. Red means off track - and you need to act.

  • On Track: ≥50% tasks done, no overdue milestones
  • At Risk: <50% done, some overdue, or due within 7 days
  • Off Track: <25% done and past due, or >50% milestones overdue
  • Color-coded health indicators across all projects
03
Team Workload

See Who's Overloaded in Seconds

The workload panel shows task distribution across your entire team - by status, by member, by urgency. Spot bottlenecks and rebalance work before deadlines slip.

  • Tasks by status: To Do, In Progress, In Review
  • Per-member task load with overload flagging (10+ active = flagged)
  • Top 5 overdue tasks sorted by urgency
  • Completion rate percentages per team member
  • Estimated vs. logged hours comparison
04
Smart Alerts

Know What Needs Attention - Right Now

Dashboard alerts surface critical issues the moment they happen. No digging through pages or checking spreadsheets - problems come to you.

  • Critical: Overdue invoices, off-track projects, failed automations
  • Warning: Stale tasks (3+ days), expiring contracts (30 days)
  • Info: Inactive clients, unassigned tasks, pending draft invoices
  • Click any alert to jump directly to the issue
  • Dismiss alerts - they reappear if the condition persists
05
Role Views

Every Role Gets Their Own Dashboard

The dashboard adapts to who's looking. Executives see the big picture. Individual contributors see their tasks. Clients see their projects and invoices.

  • Executive: Full agency overview - revenue, projects, team, alerts
  • Team Member: My tasks, upcoming deadlines, recent notifications
  • Client: Org-scoped projects, task stats, invoices, active services
  • Time-aware greeting that changes by time of day
  • Daily motivational quote from 345 curated quotes
06
Onboarding

Guided Setup for Every New User

A personalized checklist appears on first login - with role-specific steps, a progress ring, and automatic completion detection. Hit 100% and get a confetti celebration.

  • Role-specific steps (Owner, Admin, PM, Accountant, Team Member, Client)
  • Auto-detects when steps are completed
  • Progress ring with completion percentage
  • Confetti animation on full completion
  • Skip anytime - re-show from profile settings
