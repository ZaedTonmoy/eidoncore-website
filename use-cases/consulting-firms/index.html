01
The Challenge

Consulting Is Relationship-Driven - Your Tools Should Be Too

Consulting firms thrive on deep client relationships, but most project management tools treat every engagement the same. You need CRM capabilities alongside project tracking, time management alongside invoicing, and reporting alongside client communication.

  • Client relationships tracked in spreadsheets or legacy CRMs
  • Engagement scoping and billing done manually in documents
  • Consultants' utilization rates unknown until it's too late
  • Executive dashboards require exporting data from 3+ systems
02
CRM & Pipeline

Every Client Relationship in One View

Track prospects from initial inquiry to signed contract. Log meetings, calls, and emails against each client. See health scores that flag at-risk relationships. When a deal closes, instantly create an engagement project with pre-configured milestones.

  • Full client lifecycle from lead to long-term partner
  • Activity timeline: meetings, emails, proposals, contracts
  • Health scoring based on engagement activity and revenue
  • One-click project creation from won opportunities
03
Utilization & Billing

Know Your Team's Utilization Rate in Real Time

See which consultants are at capacity, who has availability, and where billable hours are being spent. Eidoncore calculates utilization rates automatically and ties every tracked hour to the right engagement and the right invoice.

  • Real-time utilization dashboards per consultant
  • Billable vs. non-billable hour categorization
  • Engagement-level profitability analysis
  • Automatic invoice generation from approved timesheets
04
Executive Reporting

Board-Ready Reports in Seconds, Not Hours

Generate executive summaries showing revenue by client, engagement health, team utilization, and pipeline forecast. No more manually pulling data from spreadsheets - Eidoncore builds the reports from the data your team already enters daily.

  • Revenue dashboards by client, engagement type, and period
  • Pipeline forecasting based on active opportunities
  • Team capacity planning and hiring insights
  • Exportable reports for board meetings and stakeholders
