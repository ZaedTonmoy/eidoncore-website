Win More Clients With Beautiful Proposals

Create professional proposals with a block-based builder, collect e-signatures, track engagement analytics, and auto-convert accepted proposals into projects and invoices - all from one platform.

01

Line Item Features

  • Manual items or import from your Service Catalog
  • Per-item quantity, unit price, discount, and tax
  • Mark items as optional - clients choose during acceptance
  • Billing type badges: Recurring, Hourly, One-time, Service
  • Document-level discount and tax overrides
  • Deposit payments - percentage or fixed amount splits
Lifecycle

Track Every Proposal From Draft to Signed

A structured lifecycle with 10 statuses keeps your pipeline organized.

Draft & Review

Build your proposal, then submit for internal review before sending. Approvers can approve or reject with feedback.

Send & Schedule

Send immediately or schedule for a specific date and time. Includes PDF attachment and public share link.

View & Track

Know exactly when clients open your proposal. Analytics show views, time spent, and device breakdown.

Accept & Sign

Clients accept with e-signature (typed or drawn). Full audit trail with name, IP address, and timestamp.

02

Negotiation Built In

  • Accept & sign - select optional items, sign with typed or drawn signature
  • Decline - with a reason sent back to the agency
  • Counter-propose - submit requested changes for agency review
  • Request extension - ask for more time before expiry
  • Send reminders - nudge clients who haven't responded
03

Conversion Options

  • To Project - create a linked project with client organization
  • To Invoice - generate a draft invoice with line items
  • To Both - project + invoice created simultaneously
  • Multi-Invoice - milestone-based payment schedule
  • Deposits - split into deposit and balance invoices
04

Service-Linked Line Items

  • Import items from your Service Catalog with pre-configured pricing
  • Recurring services auto-provision active subscriptions on conversion
  • One-time fees and recurring billing combined in a single proposal
  • Seamless handoff from proposal → invoice → payment via Stripe
Analytics

How many times the client opened your proposal.

Time to First View

How quickly the client opened after you sent it.

Time to Decision

Duration between first view and acceptance or decline.
