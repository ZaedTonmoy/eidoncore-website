Cart & Checkout for Clients

Clients browse your catalog through the branded portal, add services to cart, fill out intake forms, and pay directly via Stripe. No back-and-forth - just seamless commerce.

Tracking & Reviews

Track Delivery, Collect Reviews

Once a client purchases a service, Eidoncore tracks hours logged, credits consumed, and milestones completed. Clients can leave star ratings and written reviews, building social proof for your catalog that future clients can see.

  • Hours and credits tracking per purchase
  • Milestone-based delivery tracking
  • Client star ratings and written reviews
  • Review moderation and publishing controls
  • Service performance analytics

Build Services With Rich Detail

Each service in your catalog is a fully detailed offering. Add descriptions, features lists, cover images, categories, and intake forms that clients fill out when purchasing.

  • Rich-text descriptions with formatting
  • Feature list with bullet points
  • Cover image and thumbnail
  • Category grouping and ordering
  • Linked intake forms for requirements capture
  • Active/inactive toggle per service

Create pricing tiers within a single service - Basic, Pro, Enterprise - each with different features, prices, and deliverables. Clients choose their tier at checkout.

  • Multiple pricing tiers per service
  • Per-tier feature lists and descriptions
  • Volume discounts and client-specific pricing
  • Comparison view in the client portal
