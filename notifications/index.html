Alerts That Escalate Automatically

Notifications aren't one-size-fits-all. Eidoncore escalates alert severity over time — so overdue invoices and delayed projects get louder, not quieter.

  • Day 7 overdue → Upgraded to Critical
  • Day 30 overdue → Critical + optional Slack alert
  • Project 7+ days past deadline → Critical escalation
  • Client health: At Risk → Churn Risk → Critical
